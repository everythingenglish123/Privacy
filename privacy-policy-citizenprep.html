Privacy Policy

Citizen Prep, by Everything English with John. Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Citizen Prep mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is a study tool for the United States naturalization civics test. We designed it to collect no personal data ourselves. The free app shows advertising from Google AdMob, described below.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store, or send any personal information ourselves.
  • The app shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. The ads are non-personalized: they are chosen from general context, not from a profile of you, and the app never asks to track you across other companies' apps or websites (details below).
  • The app contains no analytics and works offline. The only internet connection it makes is to load ads.
  • Your study progress and settings are stored only on your own device.

Information stored on your device

To remember which questions you have marked as known or still learning, your practice test results, your test version, your language choice, your checklist ticks, and the state information you type in "My State" (for example the names of your governor and senators), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app. We cannot see it.

The app can export this progress as a text file or as copied text so that you can move it to another device yourself. The export is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or paste it.

Advertising

The app shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Ads are served by Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. We have configured AdMob to show non-personalized ads only. Non-personalized ads are selected from general information such as the content of the app, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and your device type; they are not based on your interests or on a profile built from your activity in other apps.

To serve and measure ads, Google may receive your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID provided by iOS or Android), your IP address, and general device information. The app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, because it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. On iOS you can reset or limit the advertising identifier in Settings; on Android you can reset or delete it under Google settings. Learn more in the Google advertising policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message before any ad is requested, as required by law. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy options" in the app's More screen.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, immigration status, application details, location, device identifiers, or usage statistics. The app has no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. The answers you practice out loud, and anything you type into the app, are never recorded or transmitted. The only third party that receives any data from the app is Google, for the advertising described above.

Links to other websites and apps

The "More" screen contains links to our other apps on the App Store or Google Play. If you tap one, you leave the app and the store's own privacy policy applies. We do not pass any information to those pages; tapping a link simply opens it.

Not affiliated with the U.S. government

Citizen Prep is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or the Department of Homeland Security. The civics questions in the app come from free, public U.S. government study materials. The app is a study aid only and is not legal advice.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults preparing for the naturalization interview. It is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the app are non-personalized for everyone.

Security

The app stores your data only on your device, so there is no personal data for us to protect on a server. Your data is protected by your device's own security. The connection used to load ads is encrypted HTTPS.

Your choices and data deletion

You can erase everything the app has saved at any time with the "Reset progress" option in the More screen, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and nothing kept off your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
