Privacy Policy

Speak Like an American, Everything English with John · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Speak Like an American mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app has been designed to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • The app does not show ads and does not use any third-party analytics.
  • Your progress, scores, saved expressions, streak and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • If the app offers an optional subscription, payments are handled by Apple or Google, and subscription status is managed by RevenueCat using an anonymous ID.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember which expressions you are learning or have mastered, your activity scores, your saved expressions, your daily streak and your settings (such as light or dark mode, sounds, and the optional first name you can type in so the app can greet you), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. The name is optional, is used only on screen, and you can leave it blank or change it at any time. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with "Reset my progress" in the More tab, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

If the app offers an optional subscription, the purchase is made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. To know whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase information from Apple or Google. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Links to other services

Some buttons in the app open other services, such as YouTube (John's video lessons and real-world video clips), the App Store, Google Play, italki, Instagram and our website. When you open them, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. The app does not send them any information about you.

Children

The app is an English learning app for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
