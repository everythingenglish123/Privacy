Privacy Policy

Master English Idioms — Everything English with John · Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Master English Idioms mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is designed to work without collecting any personal data.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information on our servers. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • Your learning progress and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • The app works fully offline. It connects to the internet only if you tap a link to an outside website.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress (for example, your flashcard ratings, completed activities, saved expressions, and daily streak) and your preferences (such as light or dark mode), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" inside the app. We cannot see it.

On Android, this local data may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account if you have backups turned on. That backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

Your name inside the app

The app may ask for a first name so it can greet you. This name is optional, is stored only on your device, and is never sent anywhere. You can change or remove it at any time in the app's More tab.

Advertising, analytics, and purchases

The app does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not offer in-app purchases. It does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

Links to other websites

The app contains links to external websites we operate or partner with (for example, our website, a lesson-booking page, and italki). If you tap one, it opens in your browser and the destination's own privacy policy applies. We do not pass any personal information to those sites; tapping a link simply opens it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of English learners aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and data deletion

Because all of your learning data is stored locally on your device, you can remove it at any time using "Clear all data" in the app's More tab, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and no personal data held by us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
