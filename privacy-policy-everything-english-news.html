Privacy Policy

Everything English News — Everything English with John · Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Everything English News mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app has been designed to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • The app collects no personal information at all. Nothing about you is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics in the app.
  • There are no purchases and no subscriptions. The app is free.
  • Your reading progress, quiz answers, saved stories, saved words and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • News stories are downloaded from a public content library we publish. No information about you is sent with those requests.

Information stored on your device

To remember which stories you have read, which reading level you prefer, your answers to the practice exercises, the stories and words you have saved, and your settings (such as light or dark mode, your translation language, and the optional first name you can type in so the app can greet you), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. The name is optional, is used only on screen, and you can leave it blank or clear it at any time. The app also keeps a copy of recently downloaded stories on your device so they load quickly and can be read offline.

This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app. We cannot see it.

How stories are delivered

The stories, definitions and exercises are not all built into the app. They are published by us as a public content library and downloaded by the app, which is how new stories appear without you having to update the app. Those files are hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2 storage), and story images may be resized for your screen by an image delivery service, wsrv.nl.

These are ordinary requests for public files, and they carry no information about you: no account, no identifier, and no record of what you read. As with opening any web page, the companies providing that hosting may log standard technical details such as the IP address making the request, under their own policies. For details, see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the wsrv.nl Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your own device. No push server is involved, no token is registered, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

Links to other websites

Each story lists the news outlets it was based on, and the app also links to sites we operate or partner with (for example our website, our YouTube channel, a lesson-booking page, an italki referral link, and our other apps in the App Store and Google Play). If you tap one, you leave the app and the destination site's own privacy policy applies. We do not pass any information about you to those sites; tapping a link simply opens it in your browser.

News content

Stories are written by us as English learning material, based on reporting from established news organizations, and every story lists its sources so you can read the original coverage. The app is a learning tool, not a news service, and we do not personalize what you see based on your behavior, because we do not track it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of English learners aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts, no purchases, no advertising, and no social or messaging features, and no personal information leaves the device.

Data sharing and security

We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. There is nothing about you for us to pass on, because the app does not collect anything about you. All network connections made by the app use encrypted HTTPS.

Your choices and data deletion

Everything the app remembers is stored locally on your device, so you can remove it at any time by using "Clear all data" in the app's settings, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and no server-side record of you to request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
