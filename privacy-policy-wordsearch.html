Privacy Policy

English Vocabulary Word Search, by Everything English with John · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the English Vocabulary Word Search mobile app ("the app"), published by John DeSarno, doing business as Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app is designed to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • Free learners see ads from Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps, and the app does not ask to track you.
  • An optional one-time purchase ("Unlock Everything") opens all lessons and removes ads. There is no subscription. Apple or Google handle the payment. RevenueCat keeps track of the purchase with an anonymous ID.
  • We do not use any analytics service of our own.
  • Your progress, answers, saved words and settings stay on your own device.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the lessons you finished, your answers, your streak, the words you saved or need to review, and your settings (language, light or dark mode, sound, reminders, and the optional first name you can type in so the app can greet you). The name is optional, is shown only on your screen, and you can leave it blank or change it at any time. The app also keeps copies of downloaded lesson pictures, audio, videos and language packs so lessons load quickly and work offline.

This data stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server, and it is removed when you uninstall the app. You can erase it at any time with "Clear all data" in the app's More screen.

Lesson files and language packs

Some lesson pictures, story videos, audio recordings and the translation packs for the 53 app languages are not built into the app. We publish them as public files on Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2 storage), and the app downloads them when you first open a topic or choose a language. Like any web request, these requests include your device's IP address, which the hosting service may keep briefly in its logs for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Unlock Everything", the app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after some finished lessons, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for extras (an extra lesson, a hint, or the day's unit games). We request non-personalized ads only, filtered for teens and adults. They are chosen from general information such as the app you are using and your approximate location (country or region), not from a profile of your interests.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect your device's IP address, device type and operating system, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (views and taps), diagnostic information about the ad software, and, on Android, the device's advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad, the app does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In regions where consent is required (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" in the app's More screen. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

People who bought "Unlock Everything" do not see ads.

Purchases

"Unlock Everything" is an optional one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and never renews or charges you again. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) handle the payment. We never see your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether you bought "Unlock Everything", the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase information from Apple or Google (the product, the purchase date and the store transaction ID). This ID is not linked to your name, email or any account. It is used only to unlock the app and to restore your purchase on a new device ("Restore purchases"). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Crash reports and ratings

We do not use a crash reporting service of our own. If you agreed to share diagnostics with Apple or Google in your phone's settings, they may send us anonymous crash reports. When the app asks for a rating, it uses Apple's or Google's own rating window; we do not see who left a rating unless you post a public review.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules them on your device with your phone's own notification system. No reminder information is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.

Links to other apps and websites

The app has links to our YouTube channel, our Instagram account, our website, our lesson booking page, our teacher page and an affiliate link on italki (if you sign up for lessons through that link, italki may pay us a commission), and our other apps on the App Store or Google Play. Opening a link takes you to that service, which has its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send any of your data to these services.

Children

The app is made for teenagers and adults learning English. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal basis (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

  • Ads: your consent, given or refused in Google's consent form. You can change it at any time in "Ad privacy choices".
  • Purchase: performance of the purchase contract (to unlock and restore it).
  • Delivering lesson files and language packs: our legitimate interest in providing the app you asked for.

Your choices and rights

  • Your learning data stays on your device. Delete it at any time with "Clear all data" or by uninstalling the app.
  • Change your ad consent with "Ad privacy choices" in the app (where available).
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK, Brazil or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to some processing. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are done on your own device. For anything else, write to us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • We do not sell personal information, and because the ads are non-personalized, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the last change. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
