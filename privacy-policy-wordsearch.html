Privacy Policy

English Vocabulary Word Search, by Everything English with John · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the English Vocabulary Word Search mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. The app has been designed to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • Free learners see ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalized: they are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps, and the app does not ask to track you.
  • An optional one-time purchase ("Unlock Everything") opens all lessons and removes ads. There is no subscription. Payments are handled by Apple or Google, and purchase status is managed by RevenueCat using an anonymous ID.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics.
  • Your progress, answers, saved words and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember which lessons you have finished, your answers to the exercises, your streak, the words you have saved or need to review, and your settings (such as your language, light or dark mode, sound, and the optional first name you can type in so the app can greet you), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. The name is optional, is used only on screen, and you can leave it blank or clear it at any time. The app also keeps a copy of downloaded lesson pictures and audio on your device so lessons load quickly and work offline.

This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app. We cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with "Clear my data" in the app's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Unlock Everything", the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a short ad after some finished lessons, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for extras (for example an extra lesson or a hint). We request non-personalized ads only. This means the ads you see are chosen based on general information such as the app you are using and your approximate location (country or region), not on a profile of your interests.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect information such as your device's IP address, device type and operating system, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (such as views and taps), and, on Android devices, the device's advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad, the app does not ask for permission to track you and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this information under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are loaded. You can review or change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy choices" in the app's settings. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

People who have bought "Unlock Everything" do not see ads.

Purchases

"Unlock Everything" is an optional one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and never renews or charges you again. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or receive your name, email address or payment details.

To know whether you have bought "Unlock Everything", the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase information from Apple or Google (such as the product purchased, the purchase date and the store transaction ID). This ID is not linked to your name, email or any account, and it is used only to unlock the app and to restore your purchase on a new device ("Restore purchases"). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

How lesson pictures and audio are delivered

Some lesson pictures, story videos and audio recordings are not built into the app. They are published by us as public files and downloaded by the app the first time you open a topic. Those files are hosted on Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2 storage). These are ordinary requests for public files; like any web request they include your device's IP address, which the hosting service may keep briefly in its logs for security and reliability. We do not receive or use these logs to identify you.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules them on your device using your phone's own notification system. No reminder information is sent to us or to any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

Links to other apps and websites

The app may show links to our YouTube channel and to our other apps (for example Master English Phrasal Verbs and Everything English News). Opening a link takes you to YouTube, the App Store or Google Play, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is designed for teenagers and adults learning English. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Because your learning data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by using "Clear my data" in the app or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can change your ad consent choice with "Ad privacy choices" in the app (where available).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Since we do not hold personal information about you, most requests can be fulfilled on your own device; for anything else, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
