Privacy Policy

-ED Pronunciation — Everything English with John · Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the -ED Pronunciation mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. We have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • The app is free, does not sell your personal information, and contains no third-party advertising.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as which screens and features you use) so we can improve the app. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites (details below).
  • Your learning progress and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • The optional "Speak It" exercise uses your device's built-in speech recognition. We never receive, record, or store your voice (details below).

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, streak, selected language, and preferences (such as light or dark mode), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" inside the app. We cannot see it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The optional "Speak It" practice lets you say a verb out loud and checks your pronunciation. If you choose to use it, the app asks for permission to use your microphone and your device's speech-recognition service. Your speech is processed by Apple's built-in speech recognition on your device; depending on your device settings, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app itself only receives the recognized text of the verb you said, uses it to show you a result, and immediately discards it. We never record, store, or transmit your voice, and no audio or transcript is ever sent to us. The microphone is used only while the exercise is active, and you can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings — every other part of the app works without it.

Analytics

To understand how the app is used and to make it better, we use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It collects anonymous usage data — for example, which screens you open, which practice activities and features you use, your selected app language, and general device information such as device model and operating-system version. Google also derives an approximate (city-level) location from your IP address.

This information is associated only with a randomly generated app-instance identifier. It is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We use it solely to see which parts of the app are useful and where we can improve. You can read more in the Firebase Privacy and Security overview and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your name inside the app

The app may ask for a first name so it can greet you. This name is optional, is stored only on your device, and is never sent anywhere. You can change or remove it at any time in the app's settings.

Links to other websites

The app contains links to external websites and apps we operate or partner with (for example our YouTube channel, our website, a lesson-booking page, an italki referral link, a "Buy Me a Coffee" support page, and our other apps on the App Store). If you tap one, you leave the app and the destination's own privacy policy applies. We do not pass your personal information to those sites; tapping a link simply opens it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of English learners aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

Other than the anonymous analytics data described above, we do not collect personal data, and we never sell your data. All network connections made by the app use encrypted HTTPS.

Your choices and data deletion

Because your learning data is stored locally on your device, you can remove it at any time using "Clear all data" in the app's More tab, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and no personal data held by us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
