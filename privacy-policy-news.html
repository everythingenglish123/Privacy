Privacy Policy

Phrasal Verb News — Everything English with John · Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Phrasal Verb News mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. We have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the app includes no third-party ads or analytics.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking added by us.
  • You can read three stories of your choice for free. After that, reading more needs an auto-renewing subscription, bought through the App Store or Google Play.
  • We never see your card details. Apple and Google take the payment. We use a service called RevenueCat only to check whether your subscription is active (details below).
  • Your reading progress, quiz answers and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • News stories are downloaded from a public content library we publish; no information about you is sent with those requests (details below).

Information stored on your device

To remember which stories you have read, your answers to the quizzes, and your preferences (such as light or dark mode), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. The app also keeps a copy of recently downloaded stories on your device so they load quickly and can be read offline. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app. We cannot see it.

Subscriptions and purchases

You can read three stories of your choice for free. To keep reading after that, you need a paid subscription. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple on iPhone and iPad, and by Google on Android. You pay them, not us. We never see or receive your card number, your billing address, or any other payment details, because that part happens inside the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies.

So that the app knows whether your subscription is active, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service that works for us as a processor. When you subscribe, restore a purchase, or open the app, RevenueCat receives:

  • an app user ID that is generated at random for your installation. It is not your name, your email address, or your Apple or Google account.
  • the purchase receipt from the App Store or Google Play, which says which plan you bought, when it started, and when it renews or expires.
  • basic technical details that travel with the request, such as the app version, the platform, and the country of your store account.

RevenueCat sends back one simple answer: subscribed or not subscribed. We use it for nothing else. It is not used to build a profile of you, to advertise to you, or to see which stories you read. For their side of it, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat keeps that subscription record for as long as the app is in the stores, so that "Restore purchases" still works when you change phones. On your own device, the app stores only a yes or no flag for whether the subscription is active.

Managing or canceling your subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them. You cancel in your own store account, not in the app:

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, then your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: the Google Play app, then your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you would also like the subscription record deleted, email us at the address at the bottom of this page and we will have it removed from RevenueCat. Apple and Google keep their own billing records under their own policies, and we cannot delete those for you.

How stories are delivered

The articles, images and quizzes are not built into the app. They are published by us as a public content library and downloaded by the app when you open it, which is how new stories appear without you having to update the app. Those files are hosted on GitHub, and story images may be resized for your screen by an image delivery service, wsrv.nl.

These are ordinary requests for public files, and they carry no information about you: no account, no identifier, no record of what you read. As with opening any web page, the companies providing that hosting may log standard technical details such as the IP address making the request, under their own policies. For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites

Each story lists the news outlets it was based on, and the app also links to sites we operate or partner with (for example our website, our YouTube channel, a lesson-booking page, an italki referral link and a support page). If you tap one, you leave the app and the destination site's own privacy policy applies. We do not pass your personal information to those sites; tapping a link simply opens it in your browser.

News content

Stories are written by us as English learning material, based on reporting from established news organizations, and every story lists its sources so you can read the original coverage. The app is a learning tool, not a news service, and we do not personalize what you see based on your behavior, because we do not track it.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience of English learners aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts, and the only information that leaves your device is the subscription information described above, which does not identify anyone by name. The app contains no advertising and no social or messaging features. It does offer a paid subscription, but the purchase is made and paid for inside the App Store or Google Play, so the store account holder controls whether any purchase can happen at all.

Data sharing and security

We never sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. The only company that receives anything tied to you is RevenueCat, and only the subscription information described above, only so the app knows whether to unlock. Apple and Google receive your payment details directly, because they are the ones taking the payment. Beyond that there is nothing about you for us to pass on. All network connections made by the app use encrypted HTTPS.

Your choices and data deletion

Everything the app remembers about your reading is stored locally on your device, so you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete. The one record that sits outside your device is the subscription record at RevenueCat described above. Email us at the address below and we will have it deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
