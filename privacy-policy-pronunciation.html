Privacy Policy

Master English Pronunciation · Everything English with John · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Master English Pronunciation mobile app ("the app"), published by Everything English with John ("we", "us"), handles information. We designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

The short version

  • The app does not require an account or any sign-in.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics.
  • Your learning progress and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • The optional "Speak It" exercises use your device's built-in speech recognition. We never receive, record or store your voice.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, streak, badges, first language, name and preferences (such as light or dark mode), the app saves this information locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or to any server, and is removed when you uninstall the app or use "Clear all data" in the app. We cannot see it.

Microphone and speech recognition

Some exercises ask you to say a word or sentence out loud. When you tap the microphone button, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service built into your device's operating system (Apple on iOS, Google on Android), and the app compares that text with the word you were asked to say. The app does not record, save or upload your voice. The speech services of Apple and Google may process audio according to their own privacy policies. If the speech service is not available, the app can record a short clip so you can listen to yourself; this clip stays in memory on your device and is deleted when you leave the screen.

You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app works without it.

Links to other websites and apps

The app has links to our YouTube channel, website, other apps, italki and Instagram. If you tap one, it opens in your browser or in that app, and their own privacy policies apply.

Children

The app is a general English learning app. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
